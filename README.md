# Watch Care Centre London — Cosmic Twilight Edition (3D WebGL & Geneva Editorial)

Independent Haute Horlogerie restoration, Witschi 6-axis chronometry, and 10 ATM hyperbaric certification flagship website for **Watch Care Centre Ltd** (`Shop G105, 13–25 Church Street, London NW8 8DT`).

## Key Highlights

- **Asymmetric Geneva Split-Screen Front Layout**: Floating mirror-glass capsule header, live `Europe/London` atelier telemetry clock, 3-tile Bento KPI strip, and a 3D Watch Cinema Vitrine (`hero-showcase-watch.mp4`).
- **3D Cinematic Calibre Studio (`Anatomy of Haute Horlogerie`)**: Interactive perspective-tilt cinema portal (`studio-suspended-calibre.mp4`) with timeline scrubbing (`0–100%`), speed modes (`1.0x`, `0.5x`, `0.75x`), 4 optical lighting grades, and 4 clickable horological inspection zones (`01`–`04`).
- **Macro Cinema Reel**: 360° celestial & micro-mechanical inspection reels (`macro-celestial-horology.mp4`) with timestamp markers and 10 ATM hydrostatic telemetry cards.
- **Global Three.js / WebGL Cosmic Backdrop**: GPU-accelerated horological micro-gears, volumetric `THREE.FogExp2` depth, and hydrostatic droplet / golden ember particle fields optimized for 60fps performance.
- **Full Atelier Sections**: Interactive Timepiece Symptom Triage, 6 Specialist Services + 5-Step Overhaul Breakdown, Specialist Brand Tabs (`Rolex`, `Patek Philippe`, `Richard Mille`), Filterable Collector Reviews, and Timepiece Assessment Booking Form.
